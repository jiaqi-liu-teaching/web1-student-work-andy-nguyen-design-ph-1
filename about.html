BIOGRAPHY

ABOUT

Korean-American artist pH-1 (Park Jun-won) is a leading voice in K-Hip Hop and R&B, renowned for his seamless blend of intricate, melodic rap flows in both English and Korean. Raised in Long Island, NY, his bi-cultural identity defines his sophisticated sound, which stands out for its introspective lyrics, emotional honesty, and positive messaging.

After moving back to Korea, he gained critical acclaim through his work with the H1GHR MUSIC label and exposure on shows like Show Me the Money. With a signature aesthetic marked by the Orange motif, pH-1 continues to expand his global reach through successful albums like But For Now Leave Me Alone and extensive world tours, cementing his status as a versatile and influential figure in contemporary music.

HIGHLIGHTS

Established Pedigree: A foundational artist in the K-Hip Hop scene, initially rising to prominence as a key member of the H1GHR MUSIC label.

Mass Recognition: Gained widespread national recognition and solidified his status following his participation in Mnet's reality show, Show Me the Money.

Critical Discography: Successfully released multiple acclaimed albums, including HALO, X, and But For Now Leave Me Alone, demonstrating consistent artistic growth and chart performance.

Global Reach: Regularly completes successful World Tours across North America and Asia, proving his strong international following and drawing power.

Brand Extension: Expanded his creative output by launching and managing his own fashion brand, EGO FETCH, reflecting his unique, clean aesthetic.
